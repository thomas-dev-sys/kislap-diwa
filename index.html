Kislap-Diwa is a 3D animated instructional film that highlights the importance of safety in the different laboratory environments of National University–Manila. Through relatable situations and realistic laboratory scenarios, the film presents common hazards, proper safety practices, emergency procedures, and the correct use of safety equipment. Created as an educational tool, it aims to help students become more aware of potential risks and encourage responsible behavior while working in the laboratory.

What is Kislap-Diwa?

Kislap-Diwa is a 3D animated instructional film developed by DND Production, a group of Information Technology students specializing in Multimedia Arts and Animation at National University–Manila, to promote laboratory safety awareness and proper safety practices. The film showcases common laboratory hazards, safety protocols, and emergency response procedures in the Computer, Chemistry, and Kitchen Laboratories through engaging visual instructions and realistic scenarios, helping students better understand and apply laboratory safety guidelines.
